# FRC Mechanical Teaching Syllabus

## Lecture 1 - Introduction to FIRST Robotics Competition
### Overview
- What is FRC? What you could learn from participation?
- FRC mechanical development timeline

### Development Cycle and Stages
- Development cycle and stage breakdown
  - Problem definition
  - Prototyping
  - Block CAD → OK2Fab CAD
  - 2D dimensioning and design for assembly
  - Assembly and testing
  - How to collaborate with software engineers
  - Iterative design process

### Competition Preparation
- Preparation and final checks before heading off to competition
  - Spare parts planning
  - Redundant or insufficient design thinking?
  - Back-up systems

## Lecture 2 - Introduction to Electrical and Mechanical Components
### Electrical Components
- Electrical components for FRC and their functional use
  - RoboRIO
  - PDH (Power Distribution Hub)
  - Radio
  - PCM (Pneumatic Control Module)
  - etc.

### Mechanical Components
- Motors (Brushed and Brushless)
- Linear Actuators (Electrical and Pneumatic)
- Power transmission systems
  - Gears
  - Pulleys
- Linear motion components
  - Sliders
- Rotation components
  - Bearings
  - Bushings

### Precision Control Systems
- Encoders
- Limit switches
- IR switches
- Optic sensors
- Servo motors

## Lecture 3 and 4 - Fundamental Mechanical System Design and Calculations
### Chassis Systems
- Chassis structure and working principles
  - Swerve drive
  - Omni drive
  - Mecanum drive
  - Tank drive (Differential drive)

### Transmission Systems
- Gearboxes
  - Single reduction ratio
  - Dual reduction ratio gearbox with shifters
    - Ball shifter
    - Dog shifter

### Specialized Mechanisms
- Climber and elevator design
  - Structure and limitations
  - Locking systems
  - Structure integrity and assembly easiness
- Turret systems
  - Various shooter design considerations
  - Turret design and combination with swerve
- Intake and gripper mechanisms
  - Various design considerations

## Lecture 5 and 6 - Full System CAD Exercise and 2D Dimensioning
### Prototyping and Design
- Prototyping techniques
- Electronics layout and wiring
  - Layout diagrams
  - Wiring holes and hollowed structures

### Material Selection
- Aluminum
- Steel
- Carbon fiber
- 3D printing materials (multiple options)

### CAD Practices
- Version control and collaboration
- Integration of subsystems

- Modular design principles

### Manufacturing Techniques
- 2D dimensioning
- Introduction to manufacturing techniques
  - CNC machining
  - Lathe operations
